# Serendipity Journal

The Journal is not a generic blog. It is the long-form editorial layer of the archive.

## Story anatomy

1. Archive metadata line
2. Large editorial title
3. Dek / thesis
4. Hero image
5. Body column
6. Pull quote / field note
7. Related archive entries
8. Sources / provenance
9. Next story

## Content types

### ESSAY
Long-form cultural writing, 10–20 minutes.

### FIELD NOTE
Short observation, 2–5 minutes.

### PHOTO STORY
Visual-first sequence with concise captions.

### INTERVIEW
Conversation with makers, designers, historians, or observers.

## Voice

Quiet, observant, specific. Avoid generic travel-copy language and decorative cultural clichés.

The Journal should repeatedly return to the Serendipity principle:

> Look closer. Notice the detail. Follow the connection.

## Editorial relationships

Every story should link to meaningful archive entities. A story about a tea bowl can lead to the bowl, Shigaraki, ceramics, kintsugi, a maker, and another story about material culture.

Never use a generic "recommended for you" block when a contextual relationship is available.

## Publication gate

Before production publication, every factual claim and image needs a source / rights record. Prototype entries must remain visibly provisional until verified.
